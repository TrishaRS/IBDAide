# IBD Aide — YouTube Demo Upload Notes

**Files:** `IBD-Aide-Demo.mp4` (53 s, 1080p) · `captions-en.srt` · `captions-es.srt`

## Upload steps

1. YouTube Studio ▸ Create ▸ Upload videos ▸ select `IBD-Aide-Demo.mp4`.
2. **Video language:** English. Audience: "No, it's not made for kids."
3. **Closed captions:** after upload, go to Subtitles (left sidebar):
   - English row ▸ Add ▸ Upload file ▸ "With timing" ▸ `captions-en.srt`
   - Add language ▸ Spanish ▸ Add ▸ Upload file ▸ "With timing" ▸ `captions-es.srt`
   Viewers then pick EN or ES from the CC menu.
4. **Audio:** the video includes an original ambient music track composed for this demo
   (synthesized from scratch — not sampled from any recording). It is fully owned by you,
   royalty-free, and safe for YouTube Content ID. No attribution needed.
